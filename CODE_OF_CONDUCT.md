# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

Be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable. Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and impact.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be
reviewed as confidentially and promptly as practical. Maintainers may remove
content or restrict participation to protect the community.
